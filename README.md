## 🌌 About Me & Core Mission

I am a 14-year-old self-taught engineer, robotics builder, and innovator from the Kashmir Valley. My mission is to converge **Mechatronics & Robotics with Generative & Agentic AI** to build **Physical AI Agents**—autonomous digital-physical entities that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world.

* 🏢 **Founder & CEO at [MAEVON](https://www.linkedin.com/company/maevon/):** Directing long-term venture development, emerging technology research, and practical innovation models.
* ⚙️ **Co-Founder & COO at Nexlyra Engineering Works:** Engineering software, embedded systems, and running practical tech internships with Peerzada Jazib (CEO).
* 📖 **Founder & Community Director at Ganderbal Reads:** Leading a non-commercial valley-wide youth literacy and knowledge-sharing movement.
* 🚀 **Innovators Circle Member at School2Startup:** Exploring frontier startup ecosystems and entrepreneurial pipelines.
* 🎓 **AI Adoption Lead at Proteios Education:** Bridging advanced AI literacy into modern educational curriculum.
* 📚 **Published Author:** Wrote and published my debut book on engineering journeys, hardware robotics, and tech philosophy (available on Amazon & Flipkart).
